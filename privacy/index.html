Privacy Policy for Atomic English

Effective date: April 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Atomic English collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Atomic English Android app.

1. Who we are

Atomic English is an Android app for learning English vocabulary through real texts, audio lessons, word cards, and learning exercises. For privacy questions, contact us at privacy@atomicenglish.app.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect or process the following information:

  • Account and authentication data: email address, one-time password sign-in status, Supabase user ID, session state, and related authentication records.
  • Learning data: words and meanings you add to learn, selected translations or meanings, learning progress, review state, settings, reset-progress actions, and account-deletion requests.
  • Dictionary and content data: dictionary pack version, refresh state, word lookup state, feedback about translations, manual translation suggestions, and support messages you choose to send.
  • Discovery lesson data: lesson state, downloaded audio state, text or transcript state, playback position, progress through a lesson, and local cache state used to let you continue learning.
  • Technical and diagnostic data: app version, build information, device/runtime state, error states, logs, and performance signals needed to keep the app working and diagnose problems.

3. Information stored on your device

Some data is stored locally on your device so the app can keep you signed in, remember settings, save learning progress, keep downloaded lesson audio available, and continue a lesson from where you stopped. Local data may include authentication session data, playback positions, learning state, dictionary pack data, cached lesson content, settings, and feedback drafts.

4. How we use information

We use information to:

  • create and maintain your account session;
  • deliver lessons, transcripts, audio, dictionary data, and learning exercises;
  • save learning progress and show the correct review state;
  • process feedback, translation reports, and support requests;
  • refresh dictionary/device-pack data;
  • diagnose app errors, improve reliability, and protect the service;
  • respond to account deletion or privacy requests.

5. Service providers and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We use service providers to operate the app, including Supabase for authentication, database, backend functions, and storage infrastructure. These providers process information only to help us provide and maintain Atomic English.

We may disclose information if required by law, to protect the security of the app, or to investigate abuse or technical failures.

6. Data retention

We keep account and learning data for as long as needed to provide the app, maintain your progress, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and operate the service. Local data may remain on your device until you delete the app, clear app data, reset progress, delete a lesson, sign out where applicable, or request account deletion.

7. Account deletion and privacy requests

The app includes an account deletion request flow in Settings. You can also contact us at privacy@atomicenglish.app to request deletion or ask questions about your data. We may need to verify your account before completing certain requests.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including authenticated access to user-owned data and backend infrastructure controls. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

Atomic English is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

10. International processing

Your information may be processed in countries other than your country of residence, depending on where our service providers operate infrastructure.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact: privacy@atomicenglish.app.
